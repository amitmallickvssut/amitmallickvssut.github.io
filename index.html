Research

Robust, delay-resilient distributed secondary control for islanded AC microgrids

Pursuing PhD research at VSSUT, Burla, developing robust, delay-resilient distributed secondary control strategies for islanded autonomous AC microgrids, focused on maintaining voltage and frequency regulation and stable power sharing under communication delay and uncertainty.
