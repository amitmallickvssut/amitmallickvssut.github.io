A Word From Me

My Philosophy

With over a decade of experience in teaching and research in electrical engineering, my work is centered on bridging rigorous power system theory with practical, real-time control solutions. As an educator, I aim to make power systems and power electronics genuinely intuitive for undergraduate students, and as a researcher, I am working to make microgrid control systems more resilient to the communication delays and uncertainties they face in real-world deployment. I take equal pride in guiding postgraduate scholars through original research and in contributing to my department through consistent administrative and academic service.
